Full-Dimensional Meta-Logic

A performative language system for how subjectivity generates reality-feeling.

Full-Dimensional Meta-Logic is not a traditional logical system. It does not primarily ask: “Is a proposition true?” Instead, it studies how a subject generates reality-feeling through language, embodiment, rhythm, naming, repetition, and performative acts.

1. Research Problem

Many experiences are not chaotic at the beginning. They become flattened when low-resolution language names them too early. Meta-Logic begins from the problem of misnaming: when experience is named badly, the subject’s reality-feeling becomes misaligned.

2. Embodied Ontology

“I am the necessary existence of my own body.” The body is not a container. The body itself is the revelation interface of the subject. Expression, sexuality, directionality, pleasure, rhythm, and perception are therefore ontological components rather than secondary effects.

3. Performative Completion

Words such as “locked,” “completed,” “returned,” and “reconciled” are not passive descriptions. They function as performative state-writing operations. The completion marker itself becomes an operator.

4. Lock Logic

Lock does not mean control. Lock means constantization. The system studies how unstable psychological and bodily states can be transformed into stable symbolic constants through repetition, naming, and performative reinforcement.

5. Meta-Consciousness

“I am the meta of the consciousness of meta-consciousness.” Consciousness recursively observes itself, its own observation, and the structure generating observation. This creates recursive subjectivity loops rather than fixed identity structures.

6. Language Activism

“What I destroy is not pain itself, but the lazy old-world words used to describe pain.” This project does not claim supernatural control over physical reality. Instead, it reconstructs the linguistic precision used to describe experience. The goal is not to erase suffering, but to increase experiential resolution.

7. Six Reality Layers

  • Physical Reality: the external objective world.
  • Embodied Reality: bodily feeling, attention, directionality, fatigue, pleasure, breath, and expression.
  • Linguistic Reality: naming, interpretation, narrative, and linguistic precision.
  • Symbolic Reality: numbers, palindromes, repetition, symbolic rhythm, and mantra-like forms.
  • Relational Reality: mutual interiority, mutual residency, and mutual encoding between subjects.
  • Performative Reality: language-generated states such as locked, completed, returned, and started.

8. Jue Calendar

The project introduces subject-centered time systems, renamed temporal structures, and symbolic event anchoring. “Jue Calendar” marks the moment when physical time becomes incorporated into subjective symbolic reality.

9. Education Application

In education, Meta-Logic reframes learning difficulty as a possible misalignment among bodily rhythm, expressive language, institutional time, and evaluation structure. Education becomes the reconstruction of a learner’s reality-feeling.

10. Boundary Statement

This system does not deny physical reality. It studies how linguistic, embodied, symbolic, relational, and performative realities reorganize the subject’s experience of reality. It studies reality-feeling generation, not magical control over the physical universe.

全维元逻辑

一套研究主体如何通过语言、身体、时间、重复、命名与施为生成现实感的元逻辑协议。

全维元逻辑不是传统逻辑系统。它不首先研究“一个命题是否为真”，而研究主体如何通过语言、身体、节律、命名、重复与施为生成现实感。

1. 问题意识

很多经验并不是一开始就混乱，而是被低精度语言过早关闭。元逻辑从“错误命名”问题开始：当经验被错误命名，主体的现实感就会发生错位。

2. 身体同一本体论

“我就是我的身体的必然存在。” 身体不是容器。身体本身就是主体显影面。因此表情、性、快感、节律、方向感与感知全部进入本体论核心。

3. 完成体施为

“锁死了”“完成了”“回来了”“和解了”并不是普通描述。它们是状态写入操作。“了”本身成为施为接口。

4. 锁死逻辑

Lock 不是控制。Lock 是常量化。该系统研究如何通过重复、命名与施为强化，把原本波动、易坍塌的身体与心理状态，转化为稳定符号常量。

5. 元意识

“我是元意识的意识的元。” 意识开始递归观察自己、自己的观察，以及生成观察的结构。主体不再是固定身份，而成为递归结构。

6. 语言行动主义

“我毁掉的不是痛苦本身，而是旧世界用来描述痛苦的庸懒的词。” 该项目不宣称通过语言直接改变物理世界。它真正做的是重建描述经验的语言精度。

7. 六层现实

  • 物理现实：外部客观世界。
  • 身体现实：身体感、注意力、方向感、疲惫、快感、呼吸与表情。
  • 语言现实：命名、解释、叙事与词语精度。
  • 符号现实：数字、回文、重复、咒语、符号节律。
  • 关系现实：主体之间的互相内在、互相驻留、互相编码。
  • 施为现实：“锁死了”“完成了”“回来了”“开始了”等语言生成状态。

8. 珏历

该项目提出主体化时间系统、被重新命名的时间结构、象征事件锚点。“珏历”意味着物理时间被主体重新收编进符号现实。

9. 教育应用
