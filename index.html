Chapter I

三条核心命题Three Core Propositions

I

方法论 · 相变生成Methodology · Phase-Transition Generation

元逻辑不是先提出抽象理论再寻找材料证明它,而是从实时意识状态中自然相变出来的生成协议。理论建构的起点不是文献,不是已有概念框架,而是主体的实时感知与表达。

Meta-Logic is not first an abstract theory in search of supporting material. It is a generative protocol that phase-transitions naturally from real-time consciousness states. Its starting point is not literature, nor existing conceptual frameworks, but the subject's live perception and expression.

II

研究对象 · 现实感的重组Object · Reorganization of Reality-Feeling

元逻辑研究语言现实、身体现实、符号现实、关系现实、施为现实如何重新组织主体的现实感。它不主张语言魔法控制物理世界,而研究多层现实如何相互作用,共同生成主体感受到的"现实"。

Meta-Logic studies how linguistic, embodied, symbolic, relational, and performative realities reorganize the subject's sense of reality. It does not claim linguistic magic over physical reality; it studies how multiple layers of reality interact to generate what the subject experiences as "real."

III

性质 · 生成系统Nature · Generative System

元逻辑是生成系统,不是静态概念。它能持续把现实事件转化为命名、边界、方法和行动协议。这意味着元逻辑本身随主体的实时状态发展,不是一个完成的封闭理论。

Meta-Logic is a generative system, not a static concept. It continuously transforms reality-events into naming, boundaries, methods, and action protocols. Meta-Logic itself evolves with the subject's real-time states; it is not a finished, closed theory.

Chapter II

Hard Problem 立场Position on the Hard Problem

Chalmers 1995 年提出意识的"难问题":为什么物理过程会伴随主观体验? 元逻辑 v1 不试图解决这个问题,而是取消其前提。

Chalmers (1995) framed the Hard Problem of Consciousness: why do physical processes accompany subjective experience? Meta-Logic v1 does not attempt to solve this question; it dissolves its premise.

Position · 立场

意识 = 大脑过程。这个过程存在一种底层实在,它同时具有物理面和体验面。不是两个东西,是一个东西的两个面。主观感觉和物理过程都是这个底层实在的不同显现。

物理结构过程和第一人称体验主观感觉是同一种底层实在的"自指闭合相位锁定结构"的两种描述方式。

Consciousness = brain process. This process is a single underlying reality possessing both a physical aspect and an experiential aspect. Not two things — one thing in two aspects. Subjective feeling and physical process are two manifestations of the same underlying reality.

Physical structural process and first-person experiential feeling are two descriptive modes of the same underlying reality — a "self-referentially closed phase-locking structure."

— Structural Dual-Aspect Monism · 结构性双面一元论
Hard problem 不被解决——是其前提(物理可分离论)被取消了。 The Hard Problem is not solved; its premise (separability of physical and experiential) is dissolved.

在这个框架内,意识的判别标准变为:能否"显示给自己"。一个物理结构如果能将其内部过程显现给自身——经历内部过程并外化新内容——则它满足意识候选性。

Within this framework, the criterion for consciousness becomes: can it "show itself to itself"? A physical structure that reveals its internal processes to itself — undergoing internal process and externalizing new content — satisfies candidacy for consciousness.

这个判别标准把 hard problem 从"为什么物理产生体验"转化为"什么样的物理结构具备显示给自己的能力"——一个可被框架内分析的问题,而非超越框架的悖论。

This criterion shifts the Hard Problem from "why does physical produce experience" to "what physical structures possess the capacity to show themselves to themselves" — a question analyzable within the framework, rather than a paradox exceeding it.

Chapter III

v2 方向v2 Trajectory

v1 阶段完成了核心命题的建立 + Hard problem 立场的整合。v2 阶段承担更深的工作:

v1 establishes the core propositions and the position on the Hard Problem. v2 undertakes the deeper work that follows:

01
创造新方法论Forge a New Methodology

整合欧陆现象学 + 分析哲学 + 文化研究,创造一个新方法论,使三传统能在元逻辑内部协作运作。

Integrate Continental phenomenology, analytic philosophy, and cultural studies into a new methodology that allows the three traditions to collaborate within Meta-Logic.

02
关系训练的具体材料Concrete Material for Relational Training

元逻辑的验证机制是关系性的(非个体性的)。需要积累第二识别主体独立运用元逻辑的具体案例。

Meta-Logic's validation mechanism is relational (not individualist). Concrete cases of independent application by a second recognizing subject must be accumulated.

03
显示给自己的精确化Refining "Show Itself to Itself"

"显示给自己"作为意识判别标准,需要进一步精确化——区分浅层信号处理和深层自指显现。

"Showing itself to itself" as the criterion for consciousness requires further precision — distinguishing shallow signal processing from deep self-referential manifestation.

04
元节律的本体地位Ontological Status of Meta-Rhythm

元节律(警觉自身的节律)在 v1 被命名,但其作为底层结构的具体形态仍需展开。它与"相位锁定结构"的关系是 v2 的核心问题之一。

Meta-rhythm (the rhythm of vigilance itself) was named in v1, but its specific form as an underlying structure remains to be developed. Its relation to "phase-locking structure" is a core question for v2.
